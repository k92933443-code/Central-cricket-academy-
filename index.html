About the academy

Where young talent learns to play the right way

Central Cricket Academy is a cricket training academy in Gomti Nagar, Lucknow, focused on developing young cricket talent through structured coaching, regular practice and a professional, disciplined training environment.

Our Mission

To give every player quality coaching, discipline and opportunities to grow in the game.

Our Vision

To nurture confident, fit and skilled cricketers who can take their game to higher levels.
